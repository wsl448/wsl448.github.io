About

I am a Ph.D. student in the Interdisciplinary Program in Artificial Intelligence at Seoul National University, advised by Prof. Sungjoo Yoo in the Computing and Memory Architecture Laboratory.

My research focuses on efficient ML systems, with an emphasis on quantization, pruning, and KV-cache compression. I am interested in reducing the memory and computation needed for large language model inference.

Research interests: Low-bit quantization · Model pruning · Memory-efficient inference
